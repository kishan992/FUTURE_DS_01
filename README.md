# 📊 FUTURE_DS_01 – Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
**FUTURE_DS_01** is a comprehensive data analytics project completed as part of the Data Science & Analytics Internship. The primary objective was to solve practical business problems by identifying revenue leaks, analyzing regional performance, and understanding customer purchasing behavior.

By transforming raw transactional data into an interactive executive-level dashboard, this project provides a roadmap for driving strategic growth and operational efficiency.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
* **Category Profitability:** The **Technology** category drives the highest net profit margins (approx. 40%), while **Furniture** shows high sales volume but lower profitability due to significant shipping and logistics overhead.
* **Regional Performance:** The **Western Region** is the top revenue generator. Conversely, the **Central Region** shows a higher rate of product returns, specifically in "Binding" and "Storage," indicating a need for quality control intervention.
* **Seasonality & Trends:** Sales data reveals a consistent **30% surge during Q4 (November-December)**. This identifying a critical window for inventory optimization and targeted marketing spend.
* **Customer Segment Growth:** While the **Consumer segment** accounts for 50% of total revenue, the **Corporate segment** has shown 15% higher year-over-year growth, representing a major scaling opportunity.

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Analysis
* **Data Cleaning:** Processed raw CSV datasets to ensure consistency in date formats, currency symbols, and geographic naming conventions.
* **Calculated Fields:** Developed custom metrics in Tableau, including **Profit Margin %**, **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth**, and **Shipping Delay Tags**.
* **Interactive Visualization:** Built a dynamic dashboard featuring cross-tab filtering, allowing stakeholders to drill down into specific Categories, Regions, and Timeframes.
