## 📌 Project Overview
**FUTURE_DS_01** is the first task completed as part of the Data Science & Analytics Internship program. The objective of this task was to analyze real-world business sales data (Superstore dataset) and transform raw data into meaningful, actionable insights using **Tableau**.

This project focuses on solving practical business problems by identifying revenue drivers and regional profitability gaps.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Addressed
* Which product categories generate the highest revenue and profit?
* How do sales trends fluctuate over time (Seasonality)?
* Which regions are most profitable, and which require strategic intervention?
* How does the relationship between sales volume and net profit vary across segments?

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Analysis
* **Data Cleaning:** Organized raw CSV data to ensure consistency in date formats and categorical naming.
* **Calculated Fields:** Developed custom Tableau calculations to derive specific business metrics.
* **Metric Selector:** Integrated dynamic parameters allowing users to toggle between **Sales** and **Profit** views.

## 📊 Dashboard Features
* **Executive KPI Overview:** Real-time metrics for Total Revenue, Profit, and Quantity.
* **Regional Revenue Analysis:** Interactive map showing geographical performance.
* **Monthly Trend Analysis:** Visualizing sales peaks and valleys over the fiscal year.
* **Category Performance Mix:** Side-by-side comparison of Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
* **Interactive Filters:** Global filters for Region, Segment, and Year for deep-dive analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
* **Regional Champion:** The **West Region** contributes the highest revenue share and profit margin.
* **Profitability Leak:** The **Furniture** category shows high sales volume but thin margins, likely due to logistics costs.
* **Q4 Momentum:** A consistent sales spike is observed in **November/December**, suggesting a strong holiday demand.
